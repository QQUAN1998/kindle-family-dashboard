### 闲置 Kindle，变成家庭看板

## English

### Give an idle Kindle a new job

For owners of idle, compatible jailbroken Kindles.

- Edit from a phone, share with family
- Weather, notes and countdowns
- Try the local demo without a cloud account

Preview locally before configuring cloud services. Device installation is a separate step.

Visual tour: https://qquan1998.github.io/kindle-family-dashboard/
